# Diagnosis — the Ben Halima / Ruqyah SA framework

How this school decides whether a person's problem is sihr (sorcery/black magic/jadoo), ayn
(evil eye/nazr), jinn (presence/possession) — or none of these — and which kind it is.
Sources: `sources/ben-halima/symptoms.txt`, `sources/ruqyah-sa/diagnosis-*.txt`,
`sources/ruqyah-sa/introduction-*.txt`, `sources/ben-halima/ruqyah-and-medicine.txt`.

> Ruqyah SA's own header: **"This diagnosis is not to be taken as medical advice."**

## 1. The three afflictions

| Affliction | What it is | Signature |
|---|---|---|
| **Sihr** (sorcery, black magic, jadoo) | Satanic incantations made by a sorcerer, often on a symbol (knots, padlock, photo, hair...), eaten, stepped on, placed in the body by jinn, or made from distance | An invisible force blocking life: abnormal, repetitive, systematic problems |
| **Ayn** (evil eye, nazr) | Effect of an admiring/jealous look or group talk "breaking" a quality, ability or blessing | Heaviness, tiredness, loss of a previously strong aptitude; babies: fever, loss of appetite/sleep, night fears |
| **Jinn** (presence or possession) | A jinn inside the body, outside it, or moving in and out; may speak/act through the person | Reactions to Qur'an, transmitted thoughts/emotions, sleep paralysis, night intrusions, localized heaviness |

They frequently occur together (sorcery is often reinforced by jinn; evil eye opens the door to
jinn), so **the school treats all three together in one program** rather than trying to
separate them (Ben Halima, THE TREATMENT §A.4).

## 2. The boundary test: normal problem vs. mystical problem

The four symptom categories below "draw a clear boundary between the normal problems of life and
those due to sorcery, jinn and/or evil eye." The common property is that the problems are
**abnormal, unexplainable in normal life, and cannot be solved by normal means**.

Differential question (Ben Halima, SYMPTOMS §17): *"Do these problems have a logical
explanation? Can we overcome them normally by efforts and means?"* If yes → treat as a normal
life problem (advice: effort, dialogue, du'a, good deeds), not as sorcery. Requests to "bring
back" a person who left, or to succeed in marriage/exams/court through unseen means, are refused
— that would itself be sorcery.

## 3. The four categories of symptoms (Ruqyah SA)

**Rule from their questionnaire page: four or more symptoms from any category ⇒ there is a
problem; start self-Ruqyah treatment or see a reliable Raaqi.**

### Category 1 — Unusual blockages in life
Repetitive, systematic, no valid reason: salaah/deeni acts feel like a battle; cannot get a job;
projects always collapse before completion; feeling tied down; cannot get married or maintain a
relationship; money always lost, no barakah; people's promises never materialize; difficulty
learning; cannot fall pregnant.

### Category 2 — Unusual mental states
Doubts about Islam or Allah; an un-overcome traumatic experience; causeless sadness/depression
up to suicidal feelings; communication problems leading to arguments/separation; over-thinking;
fears, phobias, panic/anxiety attacks; causeless or excessive anger/rage; poor concentration,
low self-esteem, indecisiveness, confusion; perpetual worry; perceiving one's own face as
pale/dark/ugly in the mirror; suspicion that people think badly of you; forgetting important
things; compulsions and addictions; feeling one is going crazy; unusual stubbornness; blanking
out mid-task; feeling people are repulsed by you; hearing buzzing/ringing/voices; seeing
movement or shadows from the corner of the eye, feeling watched.

### Category 3 — Unusual health issues
No conclusive medical diagnosis; "rare"/unnamed illness; handicapped or special-needs child;
medication has no effect; perpetually sick or stuck in one illness; stabbing/throbbing pains for
no reason; irregular/painful/excessive periods; intimacy problems, lack of libido, painful
intercourse, impotence, sterility/infertility; permanently bloated stomach; heartburn,
indigestion, irregular bowels; unexplained bruises/scratches/marks; skin issues (eczema,
psoriasis, spots); crawling sensations, pins and needles; insomnia at night with sleepiness by
day; night sweats (forehead, back, hands, feet); shoulder and lower-back pain; excessive
urination; body odour despite washing; unusual hair loss; bad breath; unusual weight loss;
tooth/bone decay; fainting or epileptic attacks; constant headaches/migraines; feeling
abnormally hot or cold; feet/calf problems; unusual eye problems.

### Category 4 — Dreams and nightmares
Recurrent, becoming systematic: blood; being chased/fights/accidents/crime; sleep paralysis;
falling sensation; snakes; crowds, letters, numbers; water/rivers/seas/tsunami/swimming; death,
dead people, living people dead, cemeteries; trees, mountains, tall buildings, flying, heights;
hair; stars; pictures; crazy people; donkeys, horses; spiders; frogs; fish; urine/stool/toilet;
idols/statues; dogs, lions, cats, predators; fire, smoke; children or babies; travelling and
vehicles; bones, teeth; sexual dreams; dolls; meat; people doing rituals; rats, pigs, disgusting
animals, animal corpses. Waking with blue marks or scratches; women experiencing rape in dreams.

## 4. Ben Halima's sorcery-effect catalogue (SYMPTOMS)

The classic presentations of sorcery, each of which can be programmed by a sorcerer:

1. **Breaking up the couple** — the most common. Two pathognomonic signs: the wife can no longer
   bear intercourse or feel pleasure; the couple argues over nothing, loving from afar but
   fighting when together, with sudden onset. Also: blocked periods/pregnancy, miscarriages,
   babies dying before birth; erectile failure only with one's spouse.
2. **Preventing work** — qualified person, every attempt inexplicably fails; or drained
   energy/ambition.
3. **Stopping studies** — can memorise but not think, forgets everything, headaches when
   studying, exam-time disasters, marks always just below the pass mark.
4. **Health problems** — sterility, impotence, and diseases with abnormal course; the doctors'
   responses follow a pattern: "nothing is wrong / we've never seen this / it's stress / we
   can't explain how you got this," and treatment doesn't work. (He holds psoriasis to be
   sorcery-contact only.)
5. **Apathy** — no motivation, constant postponing, sleeping all the time, abandoning everything.
6. **Fake love** — obsession without genuine affinity, "seeing" the person everywhere.
7. **Madness and death** — losing common sense, paranoia, talking alone, seeing things; suicide
   pushes (window, knife, pills, traffic) and repeated near-fatal accidents.
8. **Subjugation** — blind obedience to the sorcerer or the one who commissioned the work.
9. **Disobeying children** — uncontrollable mischief the child itself doesn't understand;
   inherited sorcery from the womb (weak health, rebellion, learning refusal).
10. **Other effects** — self-imprisonment (can't leave house/country); money evaporating;
    irritation; unstable personality; pathological doubt/fear/anguish; endless streak of
    misfortunes; conditional sorcery (triggers on marriage, first child, etc.).
11. **Sorcery on house, shop or car** — disputes and accidents in the house; failing business;
    breakdowns/accidents in the car.
12. **Announcement dreams** — sorcery's arrival is often announced: bitten by an animal, hurt by
    someone, endless falling, snakes, dead people, graveyards — sometimes showing the very
    person responsible.

## 5. The four delivery routes of sorcery (drives treatment choice)

| Route | Clues | Primary treatment add-on |
|---|---|---|
| **Eaten** (food/drink) | Digestive troubles in ~95%: burns, nausea, vomiting, lumps, gas, stings — permanent or episodic, medically unexplained | Senna (sana makki) + sidr herbal tea, days-long purge (see TREATMENT.md §5) |
| **Stepped on** (contact) | Skin effects, mostly legs: cuts, eczema/psoriasis that move or resist treatment; hair falling; weak legs; usually no life-blockage effects | Hijama above ankle + habba sawda oil massage + sidr |
| **Put in the body** (placed by jinn) | Localized permanent/recurrent pain (ovaries → blocked pregnancy; male organs → impotence); keeps a jinn anchored — sorcery must come out before the jinn can | Hijama on the spot + recitation |
| **Distance / symbolic** | Classic case; decoded through dreams and "key sentences" (below) | General program + the specific cancellation verse for each symbol |

Treated without a symbol search: **madness**, **sacrifice** (raw-meat dreams, lack of
personality), **statue** (mask dreams, goes with sacrifice), **revivification** (a body part
"stops responding", no conscience), **skin**, **mental weakness** (can't think or decide, lost
confidence). Also sorcery-linkable: causeless anger, repeated forgetfulness, money loss,
noises/voices/buzzing, felt presences in the house, over-passion for trivial things, eye
illness, sterility and impotence, frequent cold feeling, unexplained fears.

**Key-sentence technique:** the patient's own summary phrase names the symbol — "I keep going in
circles" → hung symbol; "I feel like a dead person" → cemetery; "I feel like I'm wearing
chains" → chains.

## 7. Evil eye specifics

Usually light and untreated: heaviness, tiredness, lack of energy. Must be treated when it
"breaks" a specific aptitude (the student who can't repeat a success, the athlete who stalls
after applause). Babies are especially susceptible (fever, appetite/sleep loss; house-jinns then
scare them through the "crack" — treat the child for ayn and the house for jinn). Group evil eye
(people talking about someone's success) breaks businesses and energy. "Bad luck", though,
indicates sorcery, not ayn.

## 8. Jinn involvement — the four reasons and their signs

1. **Sent with sorcery** (most common; in Africa, near-universal) — continuous "spoken" thoughts
   in the head, role in illness and pain; jinn may give the spouse a disgusting appearance, turn
   employers against the person.
2. **Revenge** — after accidentally harming an unseen jinn (hot water, no bismillah): beatings
   in dreams (being chased/beaten), partial paralysis, heaviness, anxiety, depression, suicidal
   thoughts.
3. **Love** — regular erotic dreams (norm for a bachelor is ~every 10–15 days; more = flag),
   blocked feeling at bedtime that lifts on waking or on Allah's name, aversion to one's spouse,
   forced episodes; jinn lovers defend "their" partner jealously.
4. **Housing** — jinn simply living in the body/house; enters through a "bridge" (sorcery, evil
   eye, or a moment of anger/sorrow/fear); may be unnoticed except heaviness where it resides.

**Reactions during Qur'an recitation (diagnostic probe, SYMPTOMS §16):** heat in the body =
sorcery burning; things "exiting" = sorcery leaving; heaviness, goosebumps, unbearability of
Qur'an, breathlessness, anxiety = jinn presence; involuntary movement or speech = jinn;
relaxation/sleep = anxiety- or fatigue-sorcery being relieved; headache = jinn in/outside head
or head-targeted sorcery; multiple reactions = combined problems; **no reaction ≠ no problem** —
if other clear symptoms exist, treat anyway (recitation is not an exclusive diagnostic).

## 9. When it is NOT sorcery — and psychotherapy

- Natural love (including of an unsuitable person), family conflicts with real causes,
  ordinary failures → not sorcery; advise normal means, du'a, patience.
- **Psychological problems**: if suffering began after a major painful event (death, assault,
  separation, failure), there is a psychological problem — which may coexist with sorcery/jinn.
  Ben Halima integrates a narrative-repetition psychotherapy (see TREATMENT.md §11) and states
  psychological "loads" are what give jinn and sorcery their grip.
- Drug-induced states: cannabis + jinn strike = stuck half-conscious delirium; jinn effect is
  treatable only after the drug stops.
- Medical framing (RUQYAH AND MEDICINE): mystical disease is suspected when pain has no origin,
  a known disease starts abnormally, or the disease is "observed, named, classified" but
  unexplained and untreatable. Headaches can just be dehydration; low-back pain can be physical
  overexertion — he explicitly checks these before ascribing them.

## 10. Intake interview (Ben Halima's recap, THE TREATMENT §C.8)

Ask in order: presenting problems and why the person suspects sorcery/jinn/ayn → is there a
logical explanation? → work situation → studies → relationships → marital status (why unmarried
if old enough; intimacy problems if married) → health (origin, medical diagnosis, digestive
problems, ovary pain, period troubles, headaches, backaches, heavy limbs, skin problems) → sleep
(waking tired? nightmares?) → dreams in detail. Note every problem, then map: digestive → eaten
sorcery; localized pains → body sorcery/jinn (hijama); skin/hair → contact sorcery (habba
sawda); dream symbols → the symbol table; and prescribe everything suspected at once ("in doubt,
we treat as if we are sure").
