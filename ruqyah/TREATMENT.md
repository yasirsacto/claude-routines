# Treatment — the Ben Halima / Ruqyah SA methodology

The complete treatment system: Ben Halima's canonical method (THE TREATMENT article ≈ his book's
treatment chapter) plus Ruqyah SA's localized 12-day self-treatment program. Sources:
`sources/ben-halima/the-treatment.txt`, `sources/ruqyah-sa/treatment-*.txt`,
`sources/ruqyah-sa/foundational-ruqyah-verses.txt`,
`sources/ruqyah-sa/specific-verses-to-be-recited-for-your-symptoms.txt`,
`sources/ruqyah-sa-self-help-handbook.txt`.

## 0. Principles

- The Qur'an cancels sorcery and evil eye and burns hostile jinn; prophetic medicine supplies
  the physical means (senna, sidr, habba sawda, olive oil, hijama, incense); practitioner
  experience supplies the combinations. The field is open — methods differ, principles agree.
- Marks of a legitimate healer: no fortune-telling, no sorcery, Qur'an only, invokes only Allah,
  no amulets, prescribes religious deeds. No "professional secrets" — teach patients everything.
- **Patient participation is decisive**: repentance encouraged (but treatment is not conditioned
  on it — "cure them" regardless; it even works on non-Muslims), no visits to seers/sorcerers
  (association breaks treatment), deprive the jinn of what it likes (sins, haram food), constant
  dhikr, counter the jinn's transmitted thoughts/emotions deliberately, preach to one's own jinn
  at bedtime.
- **Treat sihr + ayn + jinn together**; most verses are common to all three.
- **Order of operations: remove sorcery before attacking a jinn** — a jinn tied by sorcery
  cannot leave; attacking it first makes it thrash, expand in the body, and risks full
  possession. This is called "the most common mistake made by non-professionals."
- Intention (niyyah) carries the treatment: heal, destroy all magic wherever it is, return it
  to its senders.
- Don't neglect the body: vitamins/minerals for weakness, relaxation herbs (vervain, lime
  flower) for anxiety — and psychotherapy where indicated (§11).
- Gender ethics: women treat women/children; a man treating a woman requires a third person and
  strict limits; teach women hijama so they treat each other; zero tolerance for healers who
  exploit patients.

## 1. The raqi-led session (general method)

1. Patient lies down, relaxed, eyes closed, covered with a light sheet ("greenhouse effect" for
   the recitation; also modesty). Head can stay uncovered for children/asthmatics/fear.
2. **9–12 uncovered 1.5 L bottles of water** placed by the patient's head (12 now standard:
   5 suffice for ayn, 9 for jinn/up-to-5 sorceries, 12 covers hard cases). Up to 72 bottles
   (12 six-packs, open) can be prepared in one recitation for multiple patients.
3. The raqi holds the patient's temples with thumb and ring finger over the sheet (or holds/
   points at the afflicted body part), faces the bottles, and recites the full verse series
   (§3), regardless of the patient's reactions, to the end.
4. At the end, blow (with light spitting motion) over the open bottles; close them.

## 2. Using the water (the 12-day cycle)

- One bottle per day: **drink** half a litre × 3 (morning, noon, evening; at iftar/suhur in
  Ramadan) and **bathe** with the rest at sunset / early evening (jinn wake at night — "hit them
  when they wake"; in long summer days bathe ~8 pm rather than waiting for a late sunset).
- Warm, almost unbearably hot water is most effective (jinn are of fire; heat disintegrates
  them, and sorcery is of the same nature). Warm by double-boiler or microwave — don't pour the
  Koranic water through a pan directly on the stove.
- Bathing procedure: wudu first, "Bismillah," ask Allah to remove the evil and return it to its
  sender; pour over the head; (for ayn) throw the bottle behind you; wipe the water off with the
  hands. Collect the used water (washtub / towel) and dispose in nature (grass, tree, soil) —
  out of respect for the Qur'an and to avoid burning gutter-jinns; omitting this doesn't void
  the treatment. Women pause during menses (unless urgent).
- **Continuity is essential**: bathe every single day to the end. (Ruqyah SA: "Do not miss a
  single day of treatment; otherwise you will have to start from day one again." And: continue
  past 12 days until every symptom is gone.)
- For sorcery, the bath matters more than the recitation-over-patient (pre-prepared bottles
  without a session are enough); for jinn, recitation over the person is essential.

## 5. Eaten sorcery — the senna (sana makki) purge

- Grind senna leaves coarsely (not powder). Per 1.5 L bottle: 2 spoonfuls senna + 1 teaspoon
  sidr, recite over it 7× each: 7:117–122, 10:81–82, 20:68–70 (or mix with already-Koranic
  water — no recitation needed). Refrigerate (days) or freeze.
- Drink one bottle over two days, on an empty stomach: heat half a bottle ~15 min on medium,
  cool slightly, drink unfiltered, don't eat for 20–30 min. Expect diarrhea starting 3–5 h
  later; stomach pains in proportion to the sorcery present.
- **Stop criterion:** continue (spacing days out if the effect is heavy) until the infusion
  produces *only* diarrhea with no pains/discomforts — pain means sorcery is still there.
- Ruqyah SA home version: 500 ml boiling Koranic water on 1 heaped tablespoon senna, steep
  10 min, strain, drink first thing in the morning — at least 4 days within the 12-day cycle;
  senna days are the only skippable component.
- Notes: dosage down-scaled for children/light people; used with pregnant women "without
  problem" per Ben Halima; breastfeeding passes the treatment to the baby (womb-sorcery); a
  senna course is also the standard *probe* — give 1 bottle and check whether cramps accompany
  the diarrhea. (Any real agent should flag senna cautions — pregnancy, IBD, dehydration — as a
  medical question; the sources themselves don't.)

## 6. Sidr (jujube leaf)

Cleans blood and organs of sorcery and attacks jinn in the blood; advised in any blood/organ
illness and any jinn case. 1 tablespoon per 1.5 L bottle, prepared and drunk like senna; 1–3
bottles by severity; taken *after* the senna bottles when both are given.

## 7. Sorcery attached to the body — hijama (cupping)

Recitation burns body-sorcery but remnants need extraction: shave/clean site, fire-in-cup (or
machine) suction, few minutes, lift, 2 small surface incisions, re-apply until blood flow
subsides; recite the series while holding the cup or temples; blood must appear or it wasn't
done right. Practice on your own arm; no hemophiliacs; diabetics OK; doesn't break the fast.

**Placement map:** ovaries (period pain, blocked marriage/pregnancy — add kidneys if low-back
pain); above male organs + between kidneys (marital difficulties); back of head between ears
and/or base of neck (strong headaches — avoid forehead/temples: week-long marks); heart area
(aches/palpitations — for women, around the left breast per pain site); eczema/psoriasis patches
(mostly legs); above the ankle (stepped-on sorcery); anywhere with abnormal pain; jinn's seat
("a vacuum cleaner which eats ghosts"); if the jinn moves between two points, cup both; jinn
everywhere → cup everywhere. After each Koranic bath, massage water into the cupped spots.
Ruqyah SA: get hijama with simultaneous ruqyah recitation "as often as required."

## 8. Contact sorcery, skin and hair — habba sawda (black seed) oil

Cup above the ankle, hold the foot, recite; recite over a bottle of habba sawda oil; after each
bath massage wounds/pimples/eczema/psoriasis/scars with it. Also for any sorcery skin problem
and hair loss. For painful intercourse: couple applies Koranic habba sawda (or olive) oil;
plus counselling on gentleness/desire. Contact sorcery is stubborn (physically impregnated) —
expect to renew and intensify.

## 9. Ruqyah SA's 12-day self-treatment program (the localized packaging)

**Kit:** 20 L bucket of water; olive oil; senna leaves; incense sticks; "Quranic sheets" (verses
printed in edible ink — from their store/centres; recitation alone suffices without them).

**Day structure (evening, from sunset):**
1. Wudu with miswak → 2 raka'at nafl → sit in a clean place, items open, Quranic sheet into the
   bucket.
2. Recite the intentions (du'a list: cure from all health/mental/mystical/spiritual issues,
   every type of sihr/jinn/ayn, remove blockages and separation, destroy all magic wherever and
   however made, return it to its makers and commissioners, remove inherited magic, cure even
   what I don't know about) — then the general ruqyah (§3), then the specific verses for your
   symptoms (§4). Read hard, "using the Quran as a sword," with meaning in mind. Can be split
   across sessions; keep items covered between sessions.
3. Blow ×3 with light spitting on hands and body (rub from the face down), then ×3 over the
   bucket and items; remove, squeeze, and burn/bury the sheet. The water now treats everything:
   drink, cook, spray.
4. Take 1.5–2 L warmed; normal shower; then the Koranic bath: intention of ghusl cleansing sin,
   illness, sihr, ayn and jinn; wash hands to wrists; gargle and sniff; wet every part of the
   body including private parts ("it is for treatment purposes"). Collect and pour the water
   outside in a clean place.
5. Massage olive oil into hair and whole body, concentrating on symptomatic areas, reciting
   Fatiha ×7 mentally over each such area.
6. Loose garment, nothing underneath; stand over a lit incense stick 3–5 minutes.
7. 2 raka'at nafl (tahajjud intention after Isha) → du'a for shifa.
8. Senna tea in the morning (per §5), ≥4 days of the 12.
9. Drink Koranic water through the day (a few glasses; use in tea/cooking).
10. Spray the home (and business): walls, ceilings, floors, behind furniture, drawers,
    wardrobes, bathrooms (toilet pan closed/covered), saying Bismillah and reciting.

Rules: 12 days = one cycle, but **continue until all symptoms are gone**; missing the recitation
on a day is tolerable if bathing/oil/incense are done — a fully missed day restarts the cycle;
never top up the bucket — when empty, refill and re-recite everything; things may get worse
before better — don't stop for feeling better OR worse; destroy all ta'weez/amulets (soak
contents in Koranic water overnight, dry, burn; bury non-burnables; pour the water into the
garden) — treatment is void while amulets are in use; avoid sin; don't hop between healers.

## 10. Treating the jinn

- Recitation burns jinn (they shrink, weaken, suffer; killing = total consumption). Standard:
  recite + 12-day bathing; repeat as precaution. If felt in one place: hold/tap/massage the path
  head → neck → spine → kidneys → hips → leg → sole → big toe until it exits; chest-tap if it
  rises to the mouth (vomiting urge).
- **Sorcery first** (see §0). Tell the linked jinn you're only removing the sorcery so it can
  leave.
- Jinn in the brain (merged personality, controlled thoughts, possession): before recitation,
  Fatiha + Ayat al-Kursi + 3 Quls once, then a heated blade-edge (e.g. potato peeler) drawn
  lightly nape→forehead and temple→temple (slight warmth only); cups on nape/base of skull or
  behind the ears (head shaved).
- Resistant jinn (love cases): striking the soles of the feet — described as effective but Ben
  Halima *himself* prefers and promotes the candle method, and "very strongly" opposes hitting
  the face or anywhere but soles; his student's testimony in the same chapter argues against
  hitting altogether (candles work better; African beating practices caused harm).
- **Weak points:** *Fire/heat* — candles near the soles (2 per foot; patient should barely feel
  warmth, the jinn feels embers), Koranic perfumed candles lit in the house (Ayat al-Kursi ×30),
  reciting over firewood; *Salt* — salt on/massaged over the covered patient; sea water baths
  are superior (sea-side people "do not have jinns"); immersion in the sea with recitation;
  zamzam water; *Smoke* — a rolled paper with written verses, lit and blown out, smoked under
  the sheet; *Muslim jinn help* — dhikr/Qur'an by candlelight to attract them, ask Allah for
  their help, Koranic fire gives them "swords" (renew every 3–4 days); *Dream killing* — Ayat
  al-Kursi ×11 (or 30–100) + 2:148 ("aynama takunu...") before sleep, then catch and recite
  over whatever appears in the dream.
- After exit: bathe with 12 bottles + 2 sidr. If it returns, hunt the sorcery.
- **Sleep paralysis drill** (Ruqyah SA): stay calm; shape hands as if gripping it with intention
  to grab; recite mentally (Bismillah, Allahu Akbar, any Qur'an) until the tongue frees, then
  aloud — hold tight and recite Ayat al-Kursi until it dies or flees; repeat on recurrence.
- The "jinn catching" specialization (capture via a treated ex-possessed "catcher", conversion
  or destruction of the jinn, then remote destruction of the sorcery and sorcerers through it)
  is documented in `sources/ben-halima/jinn-catching.txt` and
  `sources/ben-halima/discussing-with-jinns.txt` — raqi-level technique, not self-treatment.

## 11. Psychotherapy inside ruqyah

Painful past events create "loads" that give sorcery/jinn their grip; a jinn can hide in the
trauma. Method: eyes closed, narrate the incident in present tense; at each painful sentence,
repeat it over and over until it produces no sensation; follow chains back to earlier similar
incidents; finish when only positive emotion remains ("There is nothing painful left"). Healers
should apply it to themselves. Alternate ruqyah and psychotherapy in weakened patients. Ruqyah
SA lists "psychotherapy not done" among the causes of relapse. (An agent should map this to a
recommendation of real trauma therapy — see AGENT-NOTES.md.)

## 12. House, business, vehicle, objects

Spray Koranic water everywhere (walls, roof, floor, under furniture, drawers, ovens, fridges,
washing machine, outside of windows/doors; toilet with pan closed; entrance door and marital bed
are critical for sorcery; recesses/holes/chimneys/vents for jinn — then close them with
Bismillah). Repeat Bismillah while spraying; a playing Qur'an recording lets jinn leave rather
than burn (avoids vengeance). Standalone houses: spray 2–3×, add Koranic water to cleaning
products, pour along the outside wall bases for foundation sorcery. Suspicious found objects:
protect yourself first, don't kick them, pour Koranic water over, then burn. Unsolved cases:
houses on old cemeteries or treasure sites.

## 13. Repeat attacks, enemies, and the "curse" prayers

- Serial sorcery: barrier = the protection invocations (§14) + intention of returning sorcery to
  sender; Ayat al-Kursi on seeing the sorcerer in a dream; du'a of the oppressed (ask Allah to
  avenge and stop them — or "If You have planned to guide him, guide him now; if to Hell, take
  him now"). Look for the "mother sorcery" behind serial cases and strike it.
- Defeating enemies in sleep (Ruqyah SA): 2:148 ×30–100 before sleep, ask to be shown the
  attackers in the dream, seize control, recite, destroy. Note: the figure seen isn't proof of a
  person's guilt — jinn impersonate.
- Curse salah for repeated attacks (Ruqyah SA): once per 12-day cycle at tahajjud (or 2 raka'at
  daily): 12 raka'at (2×6) Salat al-Hajah, each raka'ah after Fatiha reciting 33:68
  ("Rabbana atihim di'fayni min al-'adhab...") ×100 with cursing intention.
- "Bad day" rescue: feet (to ankles) and hands (to wrists) soaked in Koranic water (+ sidr
  powder, lime/lemon, ginger, sea/epsom salt); Falaq ×100 + Nas ×100 with removal intention.

## 14. Prevention and everyday protections

- Precautions: care with individually served food and insistent hosts; refuse lending of
  clothes/photos/handwriting; discretion about blessings; extreme courtesy toward known
  sorcerers, cut relations with close-relation sorcerers; play miserable when suspected
  sorcerers ask how you are.
- Bismillah at: entering/leaving home, eating, toilets, undressing, marital relations, closing
  doors/windows at night, throwing water/things, passing sewers; remember Allah in anger,
  sadness and fear (the jinn's entry moments).
- Ayn: "Masha'Allah / Tabarak Allah" on admiring anything (say it on others' behalf too); 3 Quls
  over the hands wiped over body/children before going out; low-key weddings; don't parade
  successes, beauty or children.
- House: no exposed images/statues with visible eyes (cover the eyes to close jinn access);
  cover food at night; no staying in major impurity; clean/ventilate disused corners.
- Daily wird (both sources): 3 Quls ×3 after Fajr and Maghrib and at sleep ("it will suffice you
  against everything"); "Bismillahi alladhi la yadurru..." ×3; "A'udhu bikalimatillahi
  at-tammati min sharri ma khalaq" ×3; Ayat al-Kursi (+3 Quls) after every salah wiped over the
  body; Ayat al-Kursi ×7 after each salah for the exposed (post-treatment, travel, marriage);
  4th kalimah ×100 morning/evening; 3rd kalimah/durud/istighfar ×100; daily Surah al-Baqarah
  recitation ("those involved in witchcraft cannot confront it"; shaytan flees the house for
  three days/nights) — or at least its last two verses ×3 nights; Yasin, Mulk, Waqi'ah, Sajdah,
  Kahf as routine.
- Raqis protect themselves: strong regular dhikr, protective intention when reciting for others,
  preventive Koranic showers for life; occasional healers: Ayat al-Kursi ×7 after each salah for
  three days after treating.

## 15. Special situations

- **Distance / travel treatment:** write the verses (Fatiha, Ayat al-Kursi, 3 Quls, the three
  anti-sorcery passages, plus any symbol verses on a separate sheet) in dissolvable ink (saffron
  / food colouring / rose water), dissolve into 12 bottles — same bathing/tea protocol. Old-
  script writing (no dots/vowels) is acceptable.
- **Someone who refuses treatment:** discreet Koranic water in food/drink to soften the sorcery
  until they consent; recite while cooking family meals (Fatiha, Ayat al-Kursi, 3 Quls, the
  three anti-sorcery passages ×7 — Arabic only; translations have no effect).
- **Proxy treatment:** an emotionally close person bathes 7 days with the intention that the
  bath reaches the victim's sorcery. Depends on intention and du'a.
- **Ramadan:** possession continues (ordinary jinn are not the chained devils); ruqyah works and
  is even blessed, only logistically harder.
- **Hard/impossible cases (set expectations honestly):** irreversible physical/mental damage
  (handicap from childhood or womb sorcery) — only relief/limitation, "do not give false hopes";
  early-childhood dominant jinn (e.g. autism as he frames it) — body has no other "master";
  patients who refuse cure or are bonded to the sorcerer; cannabis-maintained delirium until the
  drug stops. When standard treatment fails twice: repeat with new means, prayer of need,
  attention to dreams, then refer to a more experienced colleague.

## 16. Evaluating results and relapses

When sorcery and jinn go, all symptoms go (physical after-effects may need medical care —
wounds, hair, weight). Partial improvement = partial removal (multiple sorceries go gradually;
jinn leave after the sorcery). No change at all → maybe not sorcery/jinn; check application;
repeat once with adjustments; then refer. On each return visit ask: "Could I have cured all of
it the first time?" — treat everything suspected at once.

Ruqyah SA's relapse checklist: no intention; no conviction; incomplete treatment; stopping when
feeling better (or worse); hijama not done as required; psychotherapy not done; over-reliance on
the raqi; sinning during treatment; returning to previous raqis/amils; hopping between
treatments; using ta'weez; repeat attacks by magicians; seeking spirituality among people
committing shirk.
