# Symptom → Prescription Index

The lookup layer for a prescribing agent. Every row follows the school's own logic:
**presentation → what it indicates → what to prescribe.** "General program" always means: the
intake questionnaire's 4+ rule was met → intentions + general ruqyah verses + 12-day Koranic
water program (drink, bathe, oil, incense, spray home) as in TREATMENT.md §3+§9, with the listed
add-ons. Verse refs are Surah:Verse (see TREATMENT.md §4 for the exact portions and counts).
