1. Read this first

This plan sets out the Ruqyah South Africa home treatment programme so that you can carry it out yourself, at home. Everything in the plain sections below is taken from the Ruqyah SA programme.

MEDICAL CARE DOES NOT STOP
Continue with your doctors exactly as you are. Do not reduce, pause or change any medication because of this programme. Ruqyah is undertaken alongside medical treatment, not in place of it. Ruqyah SA's own material is explicit that all of this is only a means, and that the One who cures is Allah (Ta'ala).

The programme's instruction is to continue until every symptom is relieved — not to stop merely because twelve days have passed, and not to stop because things briefly feel worse (in some situations things get worse before they get better).

2. What your answers indicate

From your questionnaire: sudden causeless arguments in the marriage; recurring stomach bloating and heartburn that medicine has not explained; dreams of snakes; and episodes of being pressed down while sleeping. In the programme's own framework these fall in all four symptom categories, and specifically indicate:

  • Separation-type sihr — the classic case of al-Baqarah 2:102;
  • Eaten sihr — digestive signs are its signature; treated with senna tea (section 8);
  • Sihr made with knots — snakes in dreams are its sign; cancelled with Ta-Ha 20:26–28;
  • Night disturbance (jinn) — handled by the sleep-paralysis drill in section 9.

3. What you will need

  1. A large bucket that will hold at least 20 litres.
  2. Olive oil, or any oil that can be applied to the skin.
  3. Senna leaves.
  4. Incense sticks.
  5. Quraanic sheets (verses printed in edible ink — optional; reciting is sufficient).

4. Before anything else: clear the amulets

The treatment will not be effective while any ta'weez, amulet, protection ring or bracelet is still in use, whoever gave it. Remove them while reciting Surah Falaq, Surah Naas and Ayatul Kursi; soak the contents overnight in Quraanic water; dry and burn them; bury what will not burn; pour the used water into the garden.

7. The daily cycle (12 days minimum)

  1. Each evening from sunset: warm 1.5–2 litres of the Quraanic water; normal shower first; then the Quraanic bath with intention of cleansing from sihr, ayn and jinn — wet every part of the body.
  2. Massage olive oil into hair and body, concentrating on symptomatic areas, reciting Surah Faatihah seven times mentally over each such area.
  3. Loose garment; stand over a lit incense stick for three to five minutes.
  4. Two rakaats of nafl salaah, then dua for shifaa.
  5. Drink from the Quraanic water through the day; use it in tea and cooking.
  6. Spray the home: walls, floors, behind furniture, cupboards, bathrooms (toilet pan closed), saying Bismillah while spraying.
  7. Do not miss a day — a fully missed day restarts the cycle. Never top up the bucket: when it is finished, refill and recite the full Ruqyah over everything again.

8. Senna tea (for the digestive signs)

Pour 500 ml of boiling Quraanic water onto one heaped tablespoon of senna leaves, steep 10 minutes, strain, and drink first thing in the morning on an empty stomach; do not eat for 20–30 minutes. At least four days within the twelve, on days you can stay home (it is a laxative). Continue on later cycles until the tea produces no cramps or discomfort — only then is this component finished. If you are pregnant, breastfeeding, or have any bowel condition, ask your doctor before using senna.

9. If you are pressed down at night (sleep paralysis)

Stay calm. With your hands, make the intention to grab the thing that is on you — shape your hands as if holding it, as tight as you can. Recite whatever you know of Qur'aan in your mind (Bismillah, Allaahu Akbar, Ayatul Kursi) until your tongue is free, then recite aloud, holding tight, until it dies or flees. Repeat if it recurs.

10. Everyday protections (keep these permanently)

  • The three Quls, three times, after Fajr, after Maghrib, and at sleep — blown over the palms and wiped over the body.
  • Ayatul Kursi after every salaah.
  • Bismillah when: eating, entering/leaving home, the toilet, dressing and undressing, closing doors and windows at night, and marital intimacy.
  • Recitation of Surah al-Baqarah in the home regularly.

11. Frequently asked questions

Do I stop my medicines or my doctor's appointments?

No — never. This programme runs alongside medical care, not instead of it.

I feel worse on day three — should I stop?

No. The programme itself warns that in some situations things get worse before they get better. Continue, and note what changed for your review call.

I missed a day. What now?

A fully missed day restarts the 12-day cycle. If you at least bathed, oiled and used the incense, the day still counts even if you could not do the recitation.

Can I keep wearing my ta'weez while doing this?

No. The treatment is not effective while any amulet is in use — clear them first (section 4).

When is the treatment finished?

When every symptom is relieved — not when 12 days have passed. Repeat cycles as needed.

What if nothing changes after two full cycles?

Contact us for a full diagnosis with a Raaqi. Some cases need cupping (hijaamah) with simultaneous recitation, or in-person treatment.
