1. Read this first

This plan sets out the Ruqyah South Africa home treatment programme so that you can carry it out yourselves, at home,

for your son. Everything in the plain sections below is taken from the Ruqyah SA programme.

MEDICAL CARE DOES NOT STOP

Your son's doctors, and in particular whoever manages his seizures, must continue exactly as they are. Do not

reduce, pause or change any medication because of this programme. Ruqyah is undertaken alongside medical

treatment, not in place of it. Ruqyah SA's own material is explicit that all of this is only a means, and that the One

who cures is Allah (Ta'ala).

IMPORTANT — THE PUBLISHED PROGRAMME IS WRITTEN FOR ADULTS

Ruqyah SA's self-help programme is written throughout for an adult treating themselves. There is no published

children's version. Three parts of it therefore do not transfer directly to an eleven-year-old, and they are handled as

follows in this plan:

• 

Senna tea — withheld from this plan. The published quantity is an adult dose and there is no children's dose

given anywhere. See section 9.

• 

Hijaamah (cupping) — not a do-it-yourself step for a child. See section 9.

• 

Incense — the method is modified for safety. See section 6, step 4.

A NOTE ON MEASURING PROGRESS

The programme's instruction is to continue until every symptom is relieved, and not to stop merely because

twelve days have passed. That instruction assumes someone who can describe how they feel. Your son cannot, so

section 8 gives you a way to record what can be seen instead. Please fill it in from the first day, not later.

2. What you will need

1. 

A large bucket that will hold at least 20 litres.

2. 

Olive oil, or any oil that can be applied to the skin.

3. 

Senna leaves. (Obtain them, but do not use them yet — see section 9.)

4. 

Incense sticks.

5. 

Quraanic sheets — sheets printed with verses of the Qur'aan in edible ink that dissolves into the water. They carry

verses only: no symbols and no numbers. If you do not have Quraanic sheets, reciting is sufficient.

3. Before anything else: clear the amulets

The treatment will not be effective while any ta'weez, amulet, protection ring or bracelet is still in use, whoever gave it.

This has to be dealt with first. Because the method uses Quraanic water, make the water first (section 4), then do this:

1. 

Recite Surah Falaq, Surah Naas and Ayatul Kursi while removing the item from the person, the home or the

business.

2. 

Open the ta'weez while reciting Surah Falaq and Surah Naas.

3. 

Place all the contents into some of the Quraanic water and leave to soak overnight.

4. 

Take the contents out, let them dry, then burn them.

5. 

Anything that will not burn fully — rings, chains, nails — burn it and then bury it.

6. 

Pour the used Quraanic water into the garden.

Do the same sweep through the whole house: cupboards, the car, anything sewn into clothing, anything buried in the

yard.

4. Making the items Quraanic — the once-off session

This is done once, at the start of each round. It is the parent who performs it.

Step 1. Make wudhu with miswaak, then perform two rakaats of Nafl Salaah. Sit in a clean, pure place. Open your

bucket of water, your oil, incense and senna, and lay them out in front of you. Place one Quraanic sheet into the bucket

of water.

Step 2. Recite the verses in section 5 over everything laid out, with intention, concentration and devotion. Read with

focus, with humility and respect, in a way that you are begging Allah (Ta'ala) for assistance. Read with strength.

You may split this over more than one sitting. The recitation does not have to be completed in one go. The one

condition is that the items stay covered whenever you are not reciting over them. Set aside about an hour of uninterrupted

time, ideally after Maghrib or Esha.

Do not begin the daily cycle until the reading over everything is complete.

Never top up. Use the water and the items until they are finished. Then fill the bucket again, open fresh oil, and read the

full Ruqyah over everything again from the beginning. Never add fresh, unrecited product to something already recited

over. The simplest way to manage this is to run the items together, so that when the water finishes you open the new oil at

the same time and do one reading over the whole set.

Bismillaahir rahmaanir raheem. Qul a'oozu bi rabbin naas. Malikin naas. Ilaahin naas. Min sharril waswaasil khannaas. Allazee
yuwaswisu fee sudoorin naas. Minal jinnati wan naas.

Alongside these, recite one tasbeeh 33 times, or as many times as possible, with strong intention of breaking all magic,

hasad and evil eye, and blow with a slight spitting action onto the water, incense, oil, cosmetics, medication and food.

6. The daily cycle — twelve days

Steps 1 to 4 are done in the evening, from sunset onwards. The parent performs them for the child.

Step 1 — The Quraanic bath

Take 1.5 to 2 litres of Quraanic water from the 20 litres and warm it. Give him a normal shower or wash first. Then,

using the Quraanic water:

• 

Make the intention (niyyah) to perform ghusl and to cleanse him of illness, sihr, ayn and jinn. It does not have to be

spoken aloud. As he cannot make the intention himself, you make it on his behalf.

• 

Wash both hands up to and including the wrists.

• 

Rinse the mouth with the water and put some into both nostrils. If he cannot tolerate the nostrils, do not force it —

wet the face and move on.

• 

Wet every part of the body, leaving no hair and no part dry. Using the water on the private parts is not disrespectful;

it is for treatment, and those areas are often affected.

Wudhu afterwards is not required. Try to collect the used water and pour it outside in a clean place — stand him in a

large dish or the bath, soak the water up with a towel, and hang the towel outside to dry.

Step 2 — The oil

After the bath, apply the olive oil to his hair and over the whole body, concentrating on any area where he shows pain or

discomfort. While massaging, recite Surah Faatihah seven times, in your mind, over those parts.

Step 3 — Two rakaats and dua

Perform two rakaats of Nafl Salaah if you are able. After Esha you may make the intention for Tahajjud; you do not

need to have slept first. Then make dua for his shifaa.

ADAPTATION FOR A CHILD

Your son is not yet baligh and the obligation of salaah has not attached to him, so this step is yours to perform on

his behalf. Nothing is expected of him here.

Step 4 — The incense

As published: dress in a loose garment with nothing underneath, light an incense stick in a holder on the floor, and stand

over it for three to five minutes. Afterwards the stick can be put out and the remainder reused the next day.

SAFETY ADAPTATION — PLEASE FOLLOW THIS VERSION

Do not stand your son over a lit incense stick. With a seizure history, a fall onto a burning stick is a real risk, and

the smoke concentration at close range is heavy for a child's chest. Instead:

• 

Light the incense in a holder placed safely out of his reach and let it scent the room while he is in it, in loose

clothing.

• 

Pass his clothing and bedding through the smoke rather than holding him over it.

• 

Keep a window slightly open. If he coughs, wheezes or becomes distressed, stop and simply air the room

instead.

• 

Never leave him alone with a lit stick, even for a moment.

Step 5 — Drinking and spraying (throughout the day)

• 

Give him the Quraanic water to drink morning and evening — a few glasses across the day. Use it for tea and for

cooking.

• 

Sprinkle or spray it around the home: walls, ceilings, floors, behind furniture, doors, windows, wardrobes,

cupboards, bathrooms.

• 

Say "Bismillah" and recite Qur'aan while spraying.

• 

In the toilet, close the lid first (or cover a low pan with a board) so that the Quraanic water does not go into the pan.

That is one day. Repeat for twelve days.

On a day when the reading was not possible: if you did the bathing, the oil and the incense, that still counts as a full

day of treatment.

Do not stop because he seems better. And do not stop because he seems worse — in some situations things get worse

before they get better. Continue until the symptoms are relieved, not for twelve days only. The number of days and the

number of recitations are a guideline, and may be increased.

ON THE "DO NOT MISS A SINGLE DAY" RULE

The published programme says that missing a day means starting again from day one. Caring for a child with his

needs, some evenings will not go to plan. Rather than abandoning a round, do what you can — even the bath

alone — and speak to your Raaqi about how to count it, rather than deciding alone that the round is lost.

7. What the whole household does

• 

Surah Al-Baqarah in the home. Recite it, or play it, regularly. This is for the house, not only for him.

• 

Salaah on time. Try not to miss any prayer at its time. Children are deeply affected by the atmosphere around them.

• 

Increase Qur'aan recitation and good deeds.

• 

Turn to Allah directly. Ask forgiveness sincerely, and resolve to keep away from what is wrong. Do not rely on the

person treating him — Allah is there for everyone, not for the Raaqi only.

• 

Keep hope. Allah deals with a person according to their expectation of Him. Have good expectations and do not

despair.

• 

Do not jump between treatments, and do not return to previous Raaqis or Aamils.

9. Two things to settle before you use them

Senna tea

Senna is part of the published programme: the leaves are steeped in boiling Quraanic water and drunk first thing in the

morning on an empty stomach, on at least four days within a twelve-day span, with nothing eaten for twenty to thirty

minutes afterwards. It has a laxative effect. It is also the one part of the treatment where days may be missed.

DO NOT GIVE SENNA TO YOUR SON ON THE STRENGTH OF THIS DOCUMENT

The quantity published by Ruqyah SA is an adult quantity, and no children's quantity is given. Senna is a

stimulant laxative, and your son is taking medication. Both the amount and any interaction are questions for his

doctor and for your Raaqi — not for a printed sheet. Obtain the leaves and set them aside until you have that

answer. Everything else in this plan can begin without it.

Hijaamah (cupping)

The programme presses for hijaamah performed with Ruqyah recited at the same time, and says that if it has not been

done, it should be done as soon as possible. The therapist should be qualified, should understand sihr and jinn

possession, and should know how to perform hijaamah and Ruqyah together. Tell them upfront what it is for. The

recitation, done while the cups are on, takes about twenty minutes at most.

FOR A CHILD, THIS IS A PRACTITIONER'S DECISION

Do not arrange cupping for an eleven-year-old with an unexplained neurological condition on the basis of a

written plan. Speak to your Raaqi, and tell them his full medical picture, including the seizures and the

medication, before anything is booked.
