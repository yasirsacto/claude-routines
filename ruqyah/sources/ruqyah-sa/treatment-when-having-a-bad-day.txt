
Soak your feet
up to the ankles into the Quranic water using a foot spa or dish etc. You
can also place the hands up to the wrists in a bowl at the same time. Put some
sidr powder, lime, lemon, ginger powder, sea salt, epsom salt, into the water
if possible.

Recite surah falaq 100 times and
surah naas 100 times, with intention of removing all jinn, magic, evil, etc.,
from your body, family, home, etc.
