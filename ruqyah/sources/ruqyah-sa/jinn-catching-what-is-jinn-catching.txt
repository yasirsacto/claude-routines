
WHAT IS JINN CATCHING?

“Jinn Catching” is the practice of using certain ayaat of the Quraan to trap jinn in a human body for a short period in order to act upon the jinn (either to convert, banish or kill). It should be noted that the jinn, according to the Ruqyah SA system, are not “enslaved”, rather they are captured and released shortly thereafter.
