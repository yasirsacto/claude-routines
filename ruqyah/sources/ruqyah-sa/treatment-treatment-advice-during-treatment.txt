
THE TREATMENT HAS TO BE CONTINUED
UNTIL RELIEVED OF ALL SYMPTOMS, NOT FOR 12 DAYS ONLY. THE DETERMINATION IS UP
TO YOU.

Sinning removes the effectiveness of
treatment, and the help of Allah will not be with us if we continue sinning. This
will be no one else’s fault but our own.

Perform
all your Salaah, increase Quraan recitation and other A’maal (good deeds). If
you are going on travel, then you can continue with treatment wherever you go.

IMPORTANT: Do not miss a single day of treatment;
otherwise, you will have to start from day one again

Most important in your
treatment is your intention when doing the Ruqyah. Keep intentions
positive, strong, and focused.

Have conviction and sincerity.

Adhere to
Allah (Ta’ala’s) commands.

Adhering
strictly to the Sunnah of Nabi (Sallalahu Alayhi Wasallam).

Consumption
of Halaal and Tayyib food.

Ensure that you have read and understood the treatment
properly. Do treatment as
explained.

Do not stop treatment
because you feel better, continue treatment until evry symptom you experience
is relived completely.

Do not stop treatment
because you feel you feel worse. In some situations, things get worse before
they get better.

Continue until every
symptom is relieved and not for 12 days only.

Get cupping (hijama)
therapy with simultaneous Ruqyah recitations done as often as required.

If you have been
through any experience that still affects you, then ensure you have
psychotherapy done.

Do not rely on the
person treating you. Do proper focused treatment, Allah is there for everyone,
not the Raaqi only.

Avoid sinning; make
taubah if you do make errors.

Do not turn to previous
Raaqis and Aamils. Learn how to help yourself and take help from Allah.

Do not jump from one
treatment to another.

Remove and destroy all
ta’weez and amulets that you may have, not matter who gave it to you.
