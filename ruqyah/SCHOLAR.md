# Sheikh Ben Halima Abderraouf

The scholar behind Ruqyah South Africa's method.

## Biography

- Born **Tunis, 1967**, to a Tunisian father and an American mother; grew up in both cultures
  and embraced Islam at age fifteen.
- Moved to France in 1986 to continue studies and settled there. Statistical and economic
  engineer and Olympic mathematics champion; memorised the Qur'an on his own and earned a
  master's degree in Arabic.
- Long involvement in da'wah and tabligh from age sixteen (first three-day outing England 1983,
  first forty days Pakistan 1987, nineteen "forty days" plus a four-month outing; learned Urdu).
- Full-time Islamic work from 1995; founded a French Islamic publishing house where he wrote,
  translated and edited ~20 books, including a translation of *Hayatus Sahabah*.
- **Practicing Ruqyah professionally since 1997.** Since 2005 he spends most of the year on
  campaign: explaining Ruqyah and shirk issues, treating patients, training ruqa, and
  installing Ruqyah centres across Africa, Europe and elsewhere (Ivory Coast, Mali, Senegal,
  Tunisia, France centres are listed on his site; he reports 200+ ruqa trained and 4,000+
  patients treated in Abidjan alone in the first installation phase).
- Does **not** subscribe to amaliyyaat/ta'weez (amulet) practices that lack a footing in Qur'an
  and Sunnah. Ruqyah SA notes he sought and received review and approval of his method from
  various ulama nationally and internationally.

## His method in one paragraph

Treatment is built on the Qur'an (recitation over the patient and over water for drinking and
bathing), prophetic medicine (senna/sana makki, sidr/jujube, habba sawda/black seed oil, olive
oil, hijama cupping, incense), and accumulated practitioner experience. Sorcery (sihr), evil eye
(ayn) and jinn are diagnosed by four categories of abnormal symptoms and by dreams, then treated
together in one program. Distinctives of his school: systematic use of Koranic water baths on a
12-day cycle, verse-per-symbol cancellation of symbolic sorcery, "sorcery first, jinn second"
ordering, jinn weak points (heat/candles, salt/sea water, smoke), psychotherapy integrated into
ruqyah, and the "jinn catching" technique developed in Ivory Coast in 2007. He explicitly
opposes beating patients and teaches everything openly ("no professional secrets").

## Ruqyah South Africa's relationship to him

From their handbook (*Al-Isti'saal — Self Treatment for Patients*):

> "Ruqyah SA is an organisation of Ruqaa who have enjoyed the privilege of studying and
> practising Ruqyah under Sheikh Ben Halima Abder Raouf. We have adopted his method primarily,
> and adapted it to the South African context in terms of setting, administration and
> legalities as well as Islamic legislature and Fiqh."

- Ruqyah SA operates 7+ affiliated Ruqyah centres in South Africa, works "strictly under the
  guidance of righteous Ulama," and runs awareness, training, and treatment programs.
- Their six objectives (under Ben Halima's banner) start with: help those afflicted by nazr,
  jadoo or jinn; protect the imaan/aqeedah of Muslims from shirk; da'wah to non-Muslims who
  come for treatment.
- Site: **https://www.ruqyah.org.za** — Call centre / WhatsApp **065 555 2828**,
  info@ruqyah.org.za.
